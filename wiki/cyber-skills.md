Curated list of hacking environments where you can train your cyber skills legally and safely

## What it is
The README describes a list of sites for practising cyber skills for free, legally and safely. The main part is a table of sites with category, difficulty and description. Categories include web application security, cryptography, reverse engineering, CTF and wargames, and blue team or DFIR.

## Who it is for
Learners looking for legal places to practise offensive or defensive skills.

## Caveats
- Practice sites can be abandoned or move; verify that each one still works.
- Some entries are deliberately vulnerable apps you host yourself; keep them off shared networks.
- Only the first 20 KB of a roughly 39 KB README was reviewed.
- Overlaps with [Cyber Security University](cyber-security-university.md) and [CTF](ctf.md).
