List of CTF frameworks, libraries, resources and softwares

## What it is
The README describes a curated list of Capture The Flag frameworks, libraries, resources, software and tutorials, for beginners and seasoned players. It is split into Create (forensics, platforms, steganography, web), Solve (attacks, bruteforcers, crypto, exploits, forensics, networking, reversing, services, steganography, web) and Resources.

## Who it is for
CTF players wanting a toolbox, and organisers looking for hosting platforms.
