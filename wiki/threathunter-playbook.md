A Threat hunter's playbook to aid the development of techniques and hypothesis for hunting campaigns

## What it is
Per the README, a community project that shares detection logic, adversary tradecraft and resources to speed up detection work. Documents follow the MITRE ATT&CK structure, and the docs are published at threathunterplaybook.com and can be run via Binder.

## Who it is for
Threat hunters and detection engineers building hunting hypotheses and analytics.

## Caveats
- The README is short; the substance is in the docs site and notebooks, which were not reviewed.
- Content is tied to specific data sources and lab datasets; adapt to your own telemetry.
