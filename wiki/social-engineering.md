List of awesome social engineering resources

## What it is
A curated list of social engineering resources. Its sections cover online courses, a social engineering CTF, psychology books, social engineering books, communities, documentation, tools, a miscellaneous section and an OSINT section. Much of the content is book and course links rather than software.

## Who it is for
Cybersecurity professionals and penetration testers who want reading and course material on persuasion and human-factor attacks.

## Caveats
- The README says the material is for cybersecurity professionals and penetration testers, and for educational use in a controlled environment.
- The default branch was last committed in 2023, so expect dead links, especially among retail book URLs and older tools.
- Some entries are commercial, such as the Lucy Phishing Server.
- OSINT entries overlap with [OSINT](osint.md).
