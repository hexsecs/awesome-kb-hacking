Free educational resources that focus on learning cybersecurity by doing

## What it is
The README describes a curated list of free educational resources focused on learning by doing. It is organised as learning paths: Introduction and Pre-Security, a Free Beginner Red Team Path (tooling, crypto and hashes, web, reverse engineering and pwn, privilege escalation), a Free Beginner Blue Team Path (tools, security operations, forensics, malware), plus bonus sections on CTF practice, latest CVEs and Windows.

## Who it is for
Self-taught beginners who want a structured, free route into red or blue team skills.

## Caveats
- Some items are hosted exercises on third-party platforms, which can change or require accounts.
- Only the first 20 KB of a roughly 26 KB README was reviewed.
- Overlaps with [Cyber Skills](cyber-skills.md), which lists practice sites rather than paths.
