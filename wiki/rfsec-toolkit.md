Collection of Radio Frequency Communication Protocol Hacktools

## What it is
A collection of radio-frequency hacking tools and tutorials gathered from GitHub, YouTube and blogs. Per the README it spans SDR hardware and software (RTL-SDR, HackRF, bladeRF, USRP), GSM tools, and NFC and RFID resources.

## Who it is for
People starting in software-defined radio who want a pointer list.

## Caveats
- A Chinese-language note in the README says the author will not update it, which fits the older last commit.
- Several images are hosted on third-party sites and may no longer load.
- Transmitting or intercepting radio traffic is regulated in many places; test only with authorization and within the law.
- Cellular topics overlap with [Cellular Hacking](cellular-hacking.md).
