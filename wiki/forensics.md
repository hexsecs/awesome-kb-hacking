List of awesome forensic analysis tools and resources

## What it is
The README describes a list of free, mostly open source forensic analysis tools and resources. Sections include distributions, frameworks, live forensics, acquisition, imaging, carving, memory, network, Windows, macOS, mobile and Docker forensics, timeline analysis, and steganography. Entries carry legend markers for recommended, archived and unmaintained (2+ years) projects.

## Who it is for
Digital forensics practitioners, students and CTF players looking for open tooling by artifact type.

## Caveats
- Use acquisition and imaging tools only on evidence or systems you are authorized to examine.
- Overlaps with [Incident Response](incident-response.md) and [Malware Analysis](malware-analysis.md).
- The README's archived and unmaintained markers may not match each project's current state.
