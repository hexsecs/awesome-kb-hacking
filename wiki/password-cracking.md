Tools and resources for recovering passwords

## What it is
A curated list of tools, research and papers on password cracking and password security. Contents run through books, cloud, conversion, Hashcat (automation, distributed cracking, rules, rule tools, web interfaces), John the Ripper, miscellaneous items including notable people, websites (communities and lookup services) and wordlist tools for analysis and generation.

## Who it is for
Pentesters, red teamers and password-security researchers who audit hash strength.

## Caveats
- Cracking credentials you are not authorized to test may be illegal; use it for authorized audits and recovery only.
- Lookup services and communities may disappear or change terms.
- For wordlists, see [Probable Wordlists](probable-wordlists.md) and [SecLists](seclists.md).
