Prompt injection vulnerabilities targeting AI and LLM systems

## What it is
A curated list on prompt injection, the vulnerability where attacker-controlled text acts as instructions for an LLM. After a short introduction (which stresses indirect injection and the lack of a known general fix), it lists articles and blog posts, tutorials, research papers, tools and CTF challenges.

## Who it is for
Developers building LLM or agent features and security testers assessing them.

## Caveats
- The field moves quickly, and the README's own claims about defences should be read with that in mind.
- Attack tools and CTFs are for authorized testing and learning only.
- The upstream README was truncated in our capture, so later sections were not reviewed.
- For wider AI-risk material see [AI Security](ai-security.md).
