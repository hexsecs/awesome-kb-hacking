List of awesome Hacking tutorials, tools and resources

## What it is
The README describes a general awesome list of hacking tutorials, tools and resources, inspired by awesome-machine-learning. It is organised by area (System, Reverse Engineering, Web, Network, Forensic, Cryptography and Wargame), mostly split into tutorials, tools and general items, with a section on Docker images for penetration testing.

## Who it is for
Newcomers wanting a broad starting map of hacking topics.

## Caveats
- Broad rather than deep; narrower pages in this KB go further.
- The default branch was last committed in 2023, so entries may be stale or dead.
- Tools listed are for authorized testing only.
