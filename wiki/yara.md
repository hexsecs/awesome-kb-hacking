List of awesome YARA rules, tools, and people

## What it is
A curated list of YARA rules, tools and resources, YARA being the pattern-matching tool for malware researchers. Sections: 100 Days of YARA, Guides, Rules, Tools, Services, Syntax Highlighters, People, Videos and Talks, and Related Awesome Lists. A legend marks actively maintained, standout, new and biggest-collection entries.

## Who it is for
Malware analysts and detection engineers writing or collecting YARA rules.

## Caveats
- Third-party rule sets differ in quality and false-positive rate; test before deploying.
- Legend markers are the list maintainers' own judgement.
- Only the first 20 KB of a roughly 45 KB README was reviewed.
