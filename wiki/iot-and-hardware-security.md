Collection of tools, books, resources and software about IoT and hardware security

## What it is
The README describes a community-driven collection of tools, books and resources on IoT and hardware security. Its Testing Tools section is arranged by technique and protocol: debugging, logic analyzers and oscilloscopes, SDR, RFID/NFC, Bluetooth LE, Wi-Fi, Zigbee, LoRaWAN and cellular, MQTT, USB, CAN bus, fault injection and side channels, and firmware and reverse engineering tools.

## Who it is for
Hardware hackers and IoT testers choosing equipment and software by interface.

## Caveats
- Test only hardware and networks you own or are authorized to assess.
- Overlaps with [Embedded and IoT Security](embedded-and-iot-security.md) on device-level topics.
- Hardware tool availability and pricing change, and links may die.
