A curated list of web3 Security materials and resources For Pentesters and Bug Hunters.

## What it is
A list for pentesters and bug hunters working on web3. The README includes vulnerable web3 CTFs (Ethernaut, Damn Vulnerable DeFi and others), mind maps of smart-contract vulnerabilities and the auditor path, tools, post-mortems, crypto bug bounty platforms, newsletters, conference talks, Solidity learning resources, audit reports and certifications.

## Who it is for
Smart-contract auditors and security researchers entering blockchain security.

## Caveats
- Fast-moving field: tools and platforms listed may be outdated or defunct.
- Several mind maps are hosted on external services.
- Hands-on exploitation applies to CTFs and authorized scope only.
- Bounty platforms overlap with [Bug Bounty](bug-bounty.md).
