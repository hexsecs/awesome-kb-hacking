List of awesome reinforcement learning for security resources

## What it is
The README describes a curated list of work on reinforcement learning applied to cyber security, and says plain machine-learning-for-security work is out of scope. It is organised into Environments (simulators and gyms such as CSLE, CyberBattleSim and gym-idsgame), Papers, Books, Blogposts, Talks and Miscellaneous.

## Who it is for
Researchers who want RL training environments and papers for attack and defence automation.

## Caveats
- Narrow scope: general ML for security belongs in [Machine Learning for Cyber Security](machine-learning-for-cyber-security.md).
- The README's badges and links use a `Limmen/awesome-rl-for-cybersecurity` URL, not the Kim-Hammar repository used for this page; the relationship was not confirmed.
- Environments simulate intrusions; use them for research and training only.
- Papers and project links can go stale or move.
