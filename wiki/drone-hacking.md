List of Drone hacking tools and resources

## What it is
A curated list covering tools and research for attacking and analysing open-source, autonomous, FPV and proprietary drones. Sections include labs, CTFs and workshops, conference talks, real-time operating systems, flight controllers and embedded systems, radio and telemetry, Wi-Fi, FPV video and payloads, and BVLOS (cellular) links. The README uses a legend marking most-awesome, paid and outdated or archived entries.

## Who it is for
Security researchers and hobbyists studying UAV firmware, radio links and ground-station protocols in a lab setting.

## Caveats
- Use only on drones and radio links you own or are authorized to test; jamming, spoofing and interception tools can be illegal.
- The README itself flags some entries as outdated or archived, and links to third-party projects can go stale.
- Radio entries overlap with [RFSec-ToolKit](rfsec-toolkit.md); firmware entries overlap with [Embedded and IoT Security](embedded-and-iot-security.md).
