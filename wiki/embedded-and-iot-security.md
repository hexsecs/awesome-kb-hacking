A curated list of awesome resources about embedded and IoT security

## What it is
The README describes a list of software and hardware tools, books, research papers and case studies for embedded and IoT security. Tool sections cover analysis frameworks, extraction and support tools, and Bluetooth LE, ZigBee, SDR and RFID/NFC hardware. It also has free training, blogs, tutorials, YouTube channels and conferences, and marks commercial products with a euro symbol.

## Who it is for
Beginners (the README points them to Books and Case Studies) and practitioners who want to start firmware or device analysis with the listed frameworks.

## Caveats
- Hardware and radio tools here are for devices you own or are authorized to test.
- Overlaps with [IoT and Hardware Security](iot-and-hardware-security.md) on hardware and radio tools.
- Curated lists drift: expect some dead links, and some entries are commercial.
