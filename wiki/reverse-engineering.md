List of Reverse Engineering articles, books, and papers

## What it is
A reading list rather than a tool: a categorised set of links to software (IDA Pro, Ghidra, Binary Ninja, Capstone, Hopper), articles, books and papers. Sections include Android, Assembly, Basics, Exploitation, Instruction Sets, Mac and iOS, Malware Analysis, Network, Obfuscation, Unpacking and Windows.

## Who it is for
Learners and practitioners who want a broad reading list on reverse engineering.

## Caveats
- The README says that if links are down, archive.org may have copies, and the author offers copies of lost documents.
- The last commit is dated 2023-05-01, so expect dead links and missing newer tools.
