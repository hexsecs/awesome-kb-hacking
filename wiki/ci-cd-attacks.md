Offensive research of CI/CD systems and deployment processes

## What it is
The README describes offensive research into the systems and processes behind development and deployment. Contents are Techniques (publicly exposed sensitive data, initial code execution, post-exploitation, defense evasion), Tools, Case Studies and Similar Projects. Each entry is a link with a one-line annotation, mostly to security blog posts.

## Who it is for
Red teamers, build-pipeline engineers and DevSecOps defenders who want attack techniques to defend against.

## Caveats
- Offensive techniques; use only against pipelines you own or are authorised to test.
- Blog-post links can disappear.
- For the defensive side see [DevSecOps](devsecops.md).
