List of awesome infosec courses and training resources

## What it is
The README describes a list of information security resources and states they are for professional and educational use in a controlled environment. Sections cover MOOCs, academic courses, laboratories, capture the flag, open security books, challenges, documentation, SecurityTube playlists, and related awesome lists.

## Who it is for
People looking for courses, books and practice environments for self-directed security study.
