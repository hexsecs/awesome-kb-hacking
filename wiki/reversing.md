Collection of resources to learn Reverse Engineering from start

## What it is
The README calls itself the highest-quality resources for learning reverse engineering from start to end and links a roadmap on the bi0s wiki. Sections cover Assembly and assembly projects, OS internals, getting started, hands-on practice, deobfuscation, anti-debug and anti-analysis, C++ reversing, Windows internals blogs and symbolic execution.

## Who it is for
Beginners who want an ordered path from assembly basics toward deobfuscation and anti-analysis topics.
