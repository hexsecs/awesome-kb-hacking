List of resources for learning about vehicle security and car hacking

## What it is
The README calls this a curated list on vehicle security, car hacking and tinkering with car functionality. It has a Learn part (articles, presentations, books, papers, courses, blogs, newsletters, conferences, podcasts, who to follow), then Projects, Hardware, Software (libraries by language) and companies.

## Who it is for
Researchers and hobbyists getting into automotive security and car hacking.

## Caveats
- Safety: interfering with a vehicle can endanger people; work only on cars and test rigs you own or are authorized to test.
- Many links are talks and blog posts that may have moved.
- Hardware and wireless overlap: see [IoT and Hardware Security](iot-and-hardware-security.md).
- Only the first 20 KB of a roughly 45 KB README was reviewed.
