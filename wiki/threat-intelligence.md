List of Awesome Threat Intelligence resources

## What it is
A curated list of threat intelligence resources. The README opens with a definition of threat intelligence and is split into Sources (feeds and APIs, shown in tables, e.g. AbuseIPDB), Formats, Frameworks and Platforms, Tools, and Research, Standards and Books.

## Who it is for
Analysts and engineers building or consuming threat intelligence feeds and platforms.

## Caveats
- The README notes that analysis is needed before listed sources become true threat intelligence.
- Feeds and APIs change terms, pricing or availability; verify before depending on them.
- Indicator-focused feeds overlap with [IOC](ioc.md).
- Only the first 20 KB of a roughly 100 KB README was reviewed.
