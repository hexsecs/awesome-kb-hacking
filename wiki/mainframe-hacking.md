List of Awesome Mainframe Hacking/Pentesting Resources

## What it is
A curated list of learning material for mainframe penetration testing and security, split by platform. The IBM zSeries half has books, tutorials, scripts and tools, talks, ACF2-specific references, labs and miscellaneous items; the IBM iSeries half has its own books, tutorials and checklists, tools, talks and miscellany.

## Who it is for
Pentesters and security researchers who meet IBM zSeries or iSeries systems and need a starting reading list.

## Caveats
- Links to talks and personal blogs can rot; check before relying on one.
- Use techniques and tools only against systems you are authorized to test.
- For general pentest resources see [Pentest](pentest.md); this page is the platform-specific niche.
