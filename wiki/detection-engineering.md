Resources for designing, building, and operating detective cybersecurity controls

## What it is
The README defines detection engineering as designing, implementing and operating detective controls to identify malicious activity early, and lists resources under Concepts and Frameworks (for example MITRE ATT&CK, Palantir's alerting and detection strategy framework, and maturity models), Detection Content and Signatures, Logging, Monitoring and Data Sources, and General Resources. Entries have short annotations.

## Who it is for
SOC analysts and detection engineers building or maturing a detection programme.

## Caveats
- Mixes frameworks, rule repositories and vendor material; check each link's currency.
- Hands-on practice fits with [Detection Lab](detection-lab.md); rule-based tooling has its own pages, [YARA](yara.md) and [Suricata](suricata.md).
