List of awesome DevSecOps tools with the help from community experiments and contributions

## What it is
The README describes a collection of documents, presentations, videos, training material, tools and services supporting DevSecOps. Sections are Information (guidelines, presentations, initiatives, Wardley maps), Training (labs, vulnerable test targets, conferences, podcasts, books) and Tools (dashboards, automation, hunting, testing, alerting, threat intelligence, attack modeling, secret management). It says entries should offer free or open-source capability and commercial links are marked (P).

## Who it is for
Teams starting a DevSecOps practice who want a tool and training survey.

## Caveats
- The last commit is from October 2021, so tools and links are likely outdated or gone.
- The README admits the list is not comprehensive.
- For attacker-side material see [CI/CD Attacks](ci-cd-attacks.md).
