List of honeypot resources

## What it is
The README describes a curated list of honeypots, related components and more, split into categories such as web and services, with a focus on free and open source projects. Sections include related lists, honeypots by type, Honeyd tools, network and artifact analysis, and data tools. Order within categories reflects contribution order, not ranking.

## Who it is for
Defenders and researchers who want to deploy deception systems or study attacker behavior.

## Caveats
- Running an internet-facing honeypot can expose you to attack traffic and legal or hosting-policy issues; plan isolation first.
- Some honeypot projects listed are old or abandoned; check each project's state.
- Overlaps with [Threat Intelligence](threat-intelligence.md) for the data collected.
