Resources about the Tor network and anonymous communication

## What it is
A curated list of resources, tools and applications for the Tor network. Sections: Official Manual, Applications (Tails, Tor Browser, others), Bridges, Bypass Censorship, public reception, impact, news and legislation, State of the Onion, and Whistleblowing.

## Who it is for
People wanting to understand, use or support anonymous communication over Tor.

## Caveats
- The README states the list is independent and carries no guarantee from The Tor Project.
- Anonymity tools are dual-use, and legality varies by country; check local law.
- Not a hacking toolkit; it is mostly about privacy, censorship circumvention and the network itself.
