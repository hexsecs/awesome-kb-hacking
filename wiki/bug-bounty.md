List of Bug Bounty Programs and write-ups from the Bug Bounty hunters

## What it is
The README describes a curated collection of bug bounty programs and hunter write-ups. Its sections are Getting Started, Write Ups & Authors (blogs by named researchers), Platforms, and Available Programs. A contribution guide is linked.

## Who it is for
Newcomers choosing where to start hunting and experienced hunters looking for programs and reading material.

## Caveats
- Program scope, rewards and even existence change; confirm on the program's own page before testing.
- Some blog links are plain http URLs, and some may be dead.
- Overlaps with [Bug Bounty Reference](bug-bounty-reference.md), which sorts write-ups by bug class.
