List of resources which help during asset discovery phase of a security assessment engagement

## What it is
The README presents a curated list of resources for the asset-discovery phase of a security assessment, offensive or defensive, arguing that assets now include anything holding an organisation's data. Sections cover content discovery, IP, domain and subdomain discovery, email, network and port scanning, communication infrastructure, source-code search, cloud infrastructure, company information, internet survey data, social media and employee profiling, data leaks, and internet scan archives.

## Who it is for
Pentesters, bug bounty hunters and attack-surface teams scoping the recon stage.

## Caveats
- The list is published by a security vendor and the README promotes that vendor's attack-surface platform.
- Recon tools should only be pointed at targets you are authorised to assess, and employee-profiling sources raise privacy questions.
- Overlaps with [OSINT](osint.md) for people and company lookups.
