Cybersecurity trends, insights, and challenges from annual reports

## What it is
The README presents a vendor-neutral list of annual cybersecurity reports, limited to recurring annual publications that do not need a paid subscription. Reports are grouped into Analysis Reports (global threat intelligence, regional, sector, application, cloud, vulnerabilities, ransomware, data breaches, physical, AI) and Survey Reports (industry trends, executive perspectives, workforce). The repository stores the original documents plus Markdown conversions, which it says were produced with AI.

## Who it is for
Security leaders and analysts who want year-over-year industry reports in one place.

## Caveats
- The README says the Markdown versions are AI conversions; check the original PDF before quoting figures.
- The README tells readers to consult the original authors about licensing of report content.
- Very large (the README alone is about 360 KB) and only its first 20 KB was reviewed.
- Complements [Threat Intelligence](threat-intelligence.md) and [APT Notes](apt-notes.md) for ongoing threat reporting.
