Collection of sources of indicators of compromise

## What it is
The README describes a list of sources of indicators of compromise, meaning artifacts such as file hashes, domains, IP addresses and signatures. Sections cover indicator feeds, Snort/Suricata and YARA signature sources, IOC tools and IOC formats.

## Who it is for
Threat hunters, analysts and defenders who need feeds and formats for ingesting indicators.

## Caveats
- Indicators expire quickly; a feed listed here may be stale or discontinued, so check freshness.
- Some feeds have terms of use or rate limits.
- Overlaps with [Threat Intelligence](threat-intelligence.md).
