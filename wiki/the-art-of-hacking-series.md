## What it is
The README describes the repository as a large collection of cybersecurity references, scripts, tools, code, labs and training resources curated by Omar Santos, serving as supplemental material for his books, video courses and live training. Its directories cover cybersecurity domains, AI, certifications and more.

## Who it is for
Learners following the author's courses, and anyone wanting a wide jumping-off point into many security domains.

## Caveats
- The README describes scripts and labs as well as references.
- Breadth is large; the README is only a directory overview and folders were not individually reviewed.
- Offensive tools and labs are for authorized, lab-only use.
