List of awesome security talks

## What it is
A list of security conference talks and videos, laid out as tables by year (the README shows sections for 2016 to 2020) with conference name, location, date and a link to the video playlist or site. Maintainers named in the README are TunnelsUp, PaulWebSec and Alex Novgorodov.

## Who it is for
Anyone looking for recorded conference talks to watch or mine for topics.

## Caveats
- The newest year section in the README is 2020, so recent conferences are not covered.
- Video playlist links are prone to removal or re-hosting.
- Talks are recordings only; this is not a curriculum, see [InfoSec](infosec.md) for courses.
